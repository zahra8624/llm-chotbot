CUSTOMER SERVICE FAQ DOCUMENT

Q: What are your store hours?
A: We are open Monday-Friday 9am-6pm, Saturday 10am-4pm, closed Sunday.

Q: Do you offer free shipping?
A: Yes! We offer free shipping on orders over $50 within the US.

Q: How do I return an item?
A: Returns are accepted within 30 days with receipt. Bring items to any store location.

Q: Do you sell gift cards?
A: Yes! Gift cards are available in $25, $50, and $100 denominations. You can purchase them online or at any store location. Gift cards never expire and can be used for any products or services.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US. Express shipping is available for 1-2 business days. International orders typically take 7-14 business days depending on location.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes! We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Some restrictions may apply to certain products or countries.

Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for items in original condition with tags attached. Returns can be processed in-store or by mail using our prepaid return label. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Q: Can I exchange an item for a different size?
A: Yes! Size exchanges are free within 30 days. You can exchange in any store location or mail the item back using our return process. Online exchanges require creating a new order for the desired size.

Q: What are your holiday hours?
A: Holiday hours vary by location. We are typically closed on Christmas Day and New Years Day. Most locations have reduced hours on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and New Years Eve. Check our store locator for specific holiday hours at your nearest location.

Q: Are you open on weekends?
A: Yes! Most locations are open weekends with Saturday hours from 9am-8pm and Sunday hours from 11am-6pm. Hours may vary by location, so please check our store locator for your specific store.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and our store gift cards. For in-store purchases, we also accept cash and checks.

Q: Do you offer payment plans?
A: Yes! We partner with Klarna and Afterpay to offer buy-now-pay-later options. You can split purchases over $50 into 4 interest-free payments. Payment plan options are available at checkout for qualifying orders.

Q: How can I contact customer service?
A: You can reach our customer service team by phone at 1-800-555-0123 (Mon-Fri 8am-8pm EST), email at support@company.com, or live chat on our website. We typically respond to emails within 24 hours.

Q: Do you have a loyalty program?
A: Yes! Our rewards program is free to join and earns you 1 point for every dollar spent. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases. Members also receive exclusive offers, early access to sales, and free birthday gifts.
